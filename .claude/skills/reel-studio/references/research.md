# Swipe research method
Outlier multiplier = a reel's views ÷ that creator's typical views (or followers). 5×+ means the *idea* worked, not just the audience. Prefer outliers from small/medium accounts: their format is copyable.
For each saved reel extract: (1) hook text + formula, (2) first-3-seconds visual, (3) structure in beats, (4) length, (5) payoff, (6) CTA type, (7) why an outsider would care. Cluster by formula; a formula that appears in 3+ outliers is a pattern.
Write ideas as "<your product's fact> told with <formula> like <saved reel>". Do not copy another creator's words; reuse structure only.
